# 📊 Unemployment Analysis with Python

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) project investigating unemployment trends across Indian states before, during, and after the 2020 COVID-19 nationwide lockdown.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
- **Dataset:** [Unemployment in India (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/gokulrajkmv/unemployment-in-india)
- **Period Analyzed:** January 2020 – October 2020
- **Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Google Colab

---

## 🔍 Key Findings
1. **COVID-19 Lockdown Shock:** Average unemployment surged from ~9.76% pre-lockdown (Jan–Mar) to a peak exceeding 23% in May/June 2020 following nationwide containment measures.
2. **Most Impacted States:** Haryana, Puducherry, Jharkhand, Tripura, and Bihar recorded the highest unemployment rates during the peak lockdown window (ranging between 22% and ~29%).
3. **Labor Contraction:** A negative correlation (-0.25) was observed between employment volume and unemployment rates, reflecting informal sector disruptions and immediate workforce displacement.

---

## 💡 Policy Recommendations
- **Urban Safety Net:** Introduce an urban employment guarantee framework (similar to MGNREGA) to protect daily-wage earners from sudden economic shocks.
- **Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT):** Fast-track liquidity support and DBT relief to migrant-heavy states to prevent severe drops in household consumption.
- **Regional Reskilling & Decentralization:** Expand rural manufacturing and agro-processing clusters in high-outmigration regions (e.g., Bihar, Jharkhand) to absorb returning workers sustainably.
